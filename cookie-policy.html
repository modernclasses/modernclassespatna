1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies and browser local storage are small text files or key-value entries placed on your device when you visit a website or interact with a web-based educational service. They can be used for essential functionality (like remembering your login status) or for non-essential purposes (like advertising tracking, user profiling, and third-party analytics).

2. Our Zero-Tracking & No-Advertising Commitment

100% Tracker-Free Educational Platform:

Modern Classes Patna does NOT use Meta Pixels, Google Ads remarketing tags, TikTok pixels, Hotjar session recording, or third-party behavioral profiling trackers anywhere on our website or inside our mobile application.

Because our website does not execute any third-party marketing, analytics, or behavioral cookies, we do not expose students to unsolicited ad networks or cross-site tracking.

3. Strictly Essential Storage Audit

The table below lists the minimal storage entries used exclusively for authenticating requests and maintaining site security:

Storage Key / Identifier Classification Service Provider Purpose & Lifespan
sb-*-auth-token Strictly Essential Supabase Auth Encrypted JWT token storing current authenticated student session so you do not have to log in on every page refresh. Expires upon logout.
theme_preference Strictly Essential Modern Classes Patna Stores whether the student selected Night Study Mode (Dark Theme) or Standard Academic Theme in their local browser. Persistent until cache clear.

Note: Under EU ePrivacy Directive (Article 5(3)) and global privacy frameworks, strictly essential cookies required to provide a requested service (such as authentication) do not require advance consent banners because they are fundamental to service operation.

4. Mobile App Storage Disclosures

The Modern Classes Patna Android mobile application does not use traditional browser cookies. Instead, it utilizes secure mobile OS storage:

  • Secure Keychain / Encrypted Preferences: Stores the authenticated user session token and single-device hardware lock ID to prevent simultaneous account sharing.
  • Local Cache: Temporarily caches lecture metadata and course descriptions on device to improve load speeds and minimize cellular data consumption.

5. How to Manage or Clear Browser Storage

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings at any time:

  • Chrome / Android: Settings → Privacy & Security → Clear Browsing Data → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Safari / iOS: Settings → Safari → Clear History and Website Data.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.

Please note that clearing strictly essential authentication tokens will sign you out of your student portal session.
