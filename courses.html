Civil Services Division

BPSC 71st CCE Prelims & Mains Comprehensive Batch

Medium: Bilingual (Hindi + English)

Structured specifically for candidates preparing for the 71st Combined Competitive Examination conducted by the Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC). The curriculum encompasses foundational NCERT conceptual analysis, in-depth Bihar General Knowledge and General Studies, Current Affairs dossiers, and weekly subjective answer-writing evaluation for the Mains stage.

Syllabus Coverage

Indian Polity, Modern History of India, Bihar Special History & Culture, Geography, Indian Economy, Science & Technology, and Mental Ability.

Teaching Methodology

Daily live lectures, recorded lecture repository for revision, chapter-wise analytical discussions, and 15-year Previous Year Question breakdowns.

Evaluation & Doubts

50 full-length mock tests, sectional evaluations with negative marking simulation, printed/PDF notes, and in-app photo doubt submission.

Railway Recruitment Board
