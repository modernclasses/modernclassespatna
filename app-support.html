Support Manual

Common Support Scenarios & Practical Solutions

Follow these verified procedures to quickly resolve technical or academic queries on your mobile learning app.

1. Account Login & Single-Device Lock

Single-Device Session Policy: The app permits only one active mobile device session per student. If you log in on a new smartphone, your previous session is automatically signed out to prevent unauthorized account sharing.

Device Lockout or Changed Phone: If you replaced your smartphone and receive an "Active session detected on another device" alert:

  1. Email madhurendrayadav06@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 6209016951.
  2. Provide your registered mobile number and student name.
  3. Our administration team will verify identity and clear the prior session token within 2 business hours.

Password Reset: Tap "Forgot Password" on the login screen, enter your registered email address, and check your inbox for the Supabase authentication reset link.

2. Course Batches & Video Playback

Accessing Live Lectures: Daily live classes stream directly within the app under the "Live Classes" tab. Ensure you join at the scheduled batch time.

Video Buffering & Internet Requirements: We recommend a stable 4G/5G mobile connection or Wi-Fi with minimum 2 Mbps download speed for smooth high-definition lecture streaming.

Missed a Live Class? All daily live sessions are archived within 2 to 4 hours under your enrolled batch's "Recorded Lectures" section for anytime revision.

Course Not Showing? Swipe down on the "My Batches" screen to force-refresh the data cache from Supabase.

3. Payments & Google Play Billing Help

In-App Digital Purchases: Course enrollments purchased inside our Android app are processed securely through Google Play In-App Billing.

Payment Successful but Course Locked?

  1. Ensure you are logged into the same Google Play account that completed the purchase.
  2. Tap "Restore Purchases" in your student profile screen.
  3. If the course remains locked, forward your Google Play receipt email (containing the GPA.xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxxx order number) to madhurendrayadav06@gmail.com.
  4. Our backend verification engine will manually activate your course enrollment within 4 business hours.

4. Asking Doubts & Uploading Photos

How to Post an Academic Doubt:

  1. Open the "Doubts" tab in the app.
  2. Select the relevant subject (e.g. History, Science, Math, Bihar GK).
  3. Type your question clearly.
  4. Optionally tap the Camera icon to capture a photo of the textbook page or question.
  5. Tap "Submit Doubt".

Faculty Response Time: Our mentor team responds to doubts Monday through Saturday within 24 hours. You will receive an in-app alert when your doubt has been answered.

5. Test Series & Auto-Graded Ranks

Taking a Timed Mock Examination: Navigate to the "Tests" tab, select an active test, and tap "Start Test".

  • Ensure you do not switch apps or close the test window, as the countdown timer runs continuously.
  • Upon completion or timer expiry, your test automatically submits to the Supabase evaluation engine.
  • Instant scorecards display correct answers, negative marks, and your relative percentile among enrolled batch students.

6. Requesting Account & Data Deletion

In full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and data protection principles, registered students can permanently delete their account and associated personal data:

  1. Option A (In-App): Tap Profile → Settings → Request Account Deletion.
  2. Option B (Direct Email): Send an email to madhurendrayadav06@gmail.com with subject line "Account Deletion Request" from your registered email.

Execution Timeline: Account deletions are completed within 30 days. User credentials, attendance logs, and doubt attachments are permanently erased from the database.
