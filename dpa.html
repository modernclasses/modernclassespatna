Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

This Data Processing Agreement governs the processing of personal data when schools, educational trusts, or institutional clients contract with Modern Classes Patna for digital learning delivery.

Effective Date: October 27, 2026
Governing Standards: Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP India) & EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR Art. 28)
Applicability: Institutional Clients, Partner Schools, and Enterprise Subscribers

1. Preamble & Contractual Relationship

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms an integral part of the service contract between Modern Classes Patna (operating the educational coaching platform and Android application) and any institutional subscriber, school, university preparatory wing, or educational trust ("Customer") that purchases bulk batch access or enrolls its students/staff onto the platform.

2. Roles of the Parties

  • The Customer as Data Controller: The Customer determines the purposes and means of processing personal data relating to its enrolled students, faculty members, and guardians.
  • Modern Classes Patna as Data Processor: Modern Classes Patna processes personal data strictly on behalf of and under documented instructions from the Customer, solely for providing live streaming, recorded lectures, test evaluations, and academic doubt resolution.

3. Categories of Personal Data & Data Subjects

Personal data processed under this agreement includes:

  • Data Subjects: Enrolled students, coaching candidates, assigned mentor teachers, and administrative personnel.
  • Identification Data: Student full names, email addresses, phone numbers, assigned roll numbers, and target competitive examination goals (e.g. BPSC, Bihar SSC, Railway).
  • Academic Performance Data: Attendance logs, test answers, scores, rank percentiles, and academic doubt submissions.
  • Device & Session Tokens: Single-device hardware lock hashes and authentication session tokens used strictly to safeguard against pirated account sharing.

4. Technical & Organizational Security Measures (TOMs)

Modern Classes Patna maintains state-of-the-art security measures to safeguard institutional student data:

  • Row Level Security (RLS): Granular PostgreSQL RLS policies in our Supabase backend isolate institutional student rosters so that data can never be accessed across institutional boundaries.
  • Encryption in Transit & at Rest: All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS. Database volumes and storage buckets use AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Administrative access to student data is restricted to verified personnel with multi-factor authentication (MFA).
  • Single-Device Session Lockdown: Anti-piracy architecture ensures students cannot share institutional login credentials with unauthorized outside parties.

6. Data Incident & Breach Notification (Within 72 Hours)

Rapid Breach Incident Commitment:

In the event of a verified personal data breach affecting Customer records, Modern Classes Patna will notify the Customer's primary contact in writing without undue delay and in any event within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, providing full details of the incident and corrective remediation taken.

7. Data Deletion & Return Upon Contract Termination

Upon termination or expiration of the institutional service contract, Modern Classes Patna shall, at the Customer's written choice:

  1. Securely export and deliver a complete CSV/JSON archive of all student rosters, test performance scores, and attendance registers to the Customer.
  2. Permanently purge and delete all institutional student profile records from our active database within 30 calendar days, certifying completion in writing (excepting records required for Indian tax or regulatory accounting).
