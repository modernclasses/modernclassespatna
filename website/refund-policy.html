1. General Principles & Consumer Commitment

At Modern Classes Patna, we believe educational investments should be founded on trust, transparency, and clarity. This Refund and Cancellation Policy outlines your rights and obligations when enrolling in our coaching courses, purchasing mock test packages, or unlocking digital academic materials.

We strive to deliver exceptional pedagogical coaching for BPSC, Bihar SSC, Railway, and foundational examinations. However, because digital educational services grant instant access to proprietary lectures and copyrighted materials upon enrollment, specific conditions apply to refunds and cancellations.

2. Digital Course Batches & Instant Access

When you purchase a course batch or mock test package in the Modern Classes Patna Android app, digital access to live stream schedules, recorded archives, PDF lecture notes, and auto-graded test series is granted immediately to your student account upon payment confirmation.

Immediate Delivery Notice:

By confirming your purchase at checkout, you expressly consent to immediate digital delivery and acknowledge that once batch access is activated and educational content is accessed or streamed, standard non-delivery refund grounds no longer apply.

3. Google Play In-App Billing Purchases & Refunds

All digital in-app purchases initiated within our Android application are processed securely through Google Play In-App Billing in strict adherence to Google Play's Developer Billing Policy:

  • Self-Service Google Play Refunds (Within 48 Hours): If you made an accidental purchase, experienced a billing duplicate, or encounter technical incompatibilities within 48 hours of purchase, you can request a direct refund through Google Play's automated refund system at play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory.
  • Assisted Institute Review (After 48 Hours): If more than 48 hours have passed and you encountered a genuine service failure (such as persistent server delivery failure, duplicate charges, or verified batch cancellation by the institute), contact our administrative desk directly at madhurendrayadav06@gmail.com with your Google Play Order Number (formatted as GPA.xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxxx).
  • Evaluation Criteria: We review claims individually. If a refund is approved by our administration, batch access is revoked, and Google Play is instructed to return the payment to your original payment method.

4. European Union & International Statutory Withdrawal Rights

Under European Union consumer protection rules, consumers generally have a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal for online purchases. However, for digital content:

In accordance with Article 16(m) of EU Directive 2011/83/EU, the 14-day right of cancellation is waived when you agree that performance (the provision of digital learning content, video lectures, and syllabus materials) begins immediately upon purchase confirmation.

5. Batch Cancellations & Expiration of Access

Our courses are generally offered as fixed-duration term batches (e.g. 90-day BPSC Prelims Target or 180-day Comprehensive Foundation) rather than perpetual subscriptions:

  • One-Time Batch Fees: Course fees are one-time payments for the published batch duration. There are no surprise recurring monthly debits unless a subscription plan is explicitly selected.
  • Voluntary Discontinuation: If you decide to discontinue attending live lectures or taking tests before the batch concludes, you may cease usage at any time. Previous payments for expired or delivered curriculum periods are non-refundable.
  • Institute-Initiated Cancellation: In the rare event that Modern Classes Patna cancels a scheduled course batch prior to commencement, enrolled students will receive a 100% full refund or credit transfer to an equivalent batch at their option.

6. Direct Dispute Resolution & Chargeback Policy

We are committed to fair and prompt student support. If you experience an unexpected transaction or billing discrepancy, please reach out to us first before contacting your bank or payment provider:

Contact Us First: Most billing questions, duplicate payment concerns, or accidental purchases are resolved within 24 to 48 business hours by our Patna support team. Filing an unauthorized chargeback with your financial institution without contacting us can trigger account suspension under our security protocols.

7. How to Submit a Refund or Billing Inquiry

To request billing assistance or submit a refund inquiry, please provide the following details:

  1. Registered Student Full Name and Mobile Number.
  2. Registered Email Address used in the app.
  3. Google Play Transaction ID (e.g. GPA.1234-5678-9012-34567).
  4. Name of the enrolled Course Batch.
  5. Clear description of the technical or billing problem.
