Accessibility Statement

Modern Classes Patna is committed to ensuring our digital coaching portal and Android mobile application are accessible to learners of all abilities, including individuals with disabilities.

1. Our Commitment to Universal Educational Access

Education is a fundamental right. At Modern Classes Patna, we believe that competitive examination preparation must be accessible to every aspiring student regardless of physical, visual, auditory, or motor ability. We actively work to incorporate inclusive design principles throughout our mobile learning application and official web portal.

2. Standards & Conformance Status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.2) defines requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities. It defines three levels of conformance: Level A, Level AA, and Level AAA.

Conformance Goal:

Modern Classes Patna aims for full conformance with WCAG 2.2 Level AA standards across all student-facing mobile app screens, registration flows, and official website pages.

3. Android Mobile Application Accessibility Features

Our Flutter Android application incorporates the following built-in accessibility safeguards:

  • Screen Reader Compatibility: Standard UI components utilize accessible semantics tags compatible with Android TalkBack, reading clear descriptions for controls, scores, and class timetables.
  • Minimum Touch Target Sizes: Interactive buttons, navigation icons, and exam answer selectors adhere to a minimum 48×48 dp touch target boundary to accommodate motor-impaired learners.
  • High-Contrast Theming: Both Dark Mode (Night Study Mode) and Light Academic Theme maintain color contrast ratios exceeding 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large graphical headings.
  • Dynamic Text Scaling: The UI responds gracefully to system-wide Android font enlargement settings without breaking layout constraints or clipping exam questions.
  • Keyboard / Directional Pad Navigation: Form inputs and exam answer buttons support physical Bluetooth keyboard navigation and Tab order.

4. Website Accessibility Features

Our official website includes:

  • Skip Link: An accessible "Skip to main content" link at the very top of each page for keyboard and screen-reader users.
  • Semantic HTML5 Structure: Logical heading hierarchies (<h1> through <h3>), landmark roles (<header>, <main>, <nav>, <footer>), and descriptive alt text on all educational imagery.
  • Focus Indicators: Clear, high-visibility focus outlines on all interactive links, form inputs, and buttons.

5. Known Limitations & Technical Alternatives

Despite our efforts, some legacy or third-party educational materials may have accessibility limitations:

  • Handwritten Doubt Attachments: Student-uploaded photos of handwritten notes or complex mathematical equations in the Doubt module may lack automated text transcripts. In such cases, faculty mentors provide typed explanations in response.
  • Live Video Streams: Real-time live classroom lectures currently rely on spoken Hindi/Bilingual audio without instant automated live captions. However, recorded lecture archives are scheduled for automated transcript indexing.
