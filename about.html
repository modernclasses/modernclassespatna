About Modern Classes Patna

A dedicated educational coaching organization established to provide disciplined instruction, high-quality bilingual study materials, and sincere academic guidance for aspirants in Patna and across Bihar.

Who We Are

Dedicated Educational Coaching in Bihar

Modern Classes Patna is an educational coaching institute headquartered in Patna, Bihar. Our core mission is to empower students with the analytical understanding, factual command, and problem-solving speed required to succeed in competitive examinations and academic curricula.

Operating in key educational hubs in Patna including Kumhrar, Musallahpur Hat, and Boring Road, we blend traditional classroom teaching with modern digital access via our dedicated Android learning app. This enables students to attend daily live lectures, review recorded archives, access curated PDF notes, and participate in auto-graded mock tests from any location.

"Our focus is not on shortcuts or unrealistic guarantees, but on consistent effort, structured syllabus coverage, and rigorous practice."

Our Foundational Principles

  • ✓

  • ✓
