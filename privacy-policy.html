3. Android Device Permissions & Legitimate Use

The Modern Classes Patna Android application requests only permissions essential for educational features. These permissions are requested at the point of need with user confirmation:

  • Camera Permission (`android.permission.CAMERA`): Required exclusively if a student chooses to take a direct photograph of a textbook problem, handwritten equation, or syllabus page to submit in the Doubt Resolution section. The camera is never accessed in the background.
  • Storage / Media Images (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`): Required only when a student chooses to upload an existing problem screenshot or PDF document from their photo library into the doubt module or set their profile photo.
  • Push Notifications (`android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): Used via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver operational notices: live class start reminders, urgent batch timing updates, and newly graded mock test releases. Students can disable notifications at any time in their device settings.
  • Internet & Network State: Required to connect to the Supabase database API, stream video lectures, and verify Google Play purchase receipts.

Explicit Clarification on Non-Collected Data: The Modern Classes Patna application does NOT access or collect device contact books, call logs, SMS message content, GPS precise physical location, or microphone audio in the background.

4. How We Use Student Information

We process collected data strictly for the following legitimate educational purposes:

  1. To provide access to subscribed courses, lectures, PDF notes, and test series.
  2. To authenticate students and enforce our single-device anti-piracy session protection.
  3. To evaluate mock tests, calculate instant scorecards, and provide rank analyses.
  4. To enable faculty members to reply to academic doubts.
  5. To communicate important exam notifications, batch changes, or fee receipts.
  6. To verify Google Play digital purchases and maintain transaction audit records.

No Third-Party Advertising: Modern Classes Patna does not display third-party advertisements inside the application. We do not sell, rent, or trade student profiles to commercial marketing networks.

5. Data Storage, Row Level Security & Encryption

Our backend architecture leverages Supabase (PostgreSQL) with strict enterprise security protocols:

  • Row Level Security (RLS): Every database table (`profiles`, `enrollments`, `test_submissions`, `doubts`, `attendance`) is secured with granular RLS policies. A student can only view and modify their own personal data; teachers can access only the rosters of batches to which they are officially assigned; master administrative access is restricted to verified institute personnel.
  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the mobile application and backend APIs are encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • Access Isolation: Database access credentials and API service keys are securely managed through environment variables and edge functions; no administrative keys are hardcoded in the client application.

6. Payments & Google Play Billing Disclosures

Modern Classes Patna complies fully with Google Play's Developer Billing Policy for digital content:

  • Digital learning content, mock test series, and mobile course enrollments offered through the Android app are processed securely via Google Play In-App Billing.
  • All financial data (credit cards, debit cards, UPI credentials, net banking details) is handled exclusively by Google Payment Services. Modern Classes Patna never sees, stores, or transmits raw banking credentials.
  • Purchase verification is performed cryptographically via our secure Supabase Edge Function (`verify_purchase`), which checks the purchase token directly against Google Play Developer API before granting batch enrollment.
  • Refund requests for digital in-app purchases are handled in accordance with Google Play's standard refund policy. Students experiencing billing discrepancies may also contact our support desk directly at madhurendrayadav06@gmail.com.

8. User Rights & Account Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play Developer policies and global data protection principles, every registered student possesses the following rights:

  • Right to Access & Rectify: Students may review and update their profile display name and target exam preferences through the in-app profile screen.
  • Right to Account & Data Deletion: Students have the absolute right to request complete deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

Students can request account deletion through either of the following methods:

  1. Inside the App: Navigate to Profile → Settings → Request Account Deletion.
  2. Via Official Email: Send an email from your registered email address to madhurendrayadav06@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - Modern Classes Patna", specifying your registered mobile number and name.

Timeline: Account deletion requests are verified and processed within 30 days. Upon processing, your user profile, authentication records, attendance history, and doubt attachments are permanently purged from our active Supabase database. Anonymized financial transaction tokens may be retained only for legal tax and audit compliance required by Indian law.
